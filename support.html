If the alarm didn't go off

This is the one thing the app has to get right, so please tell us if it ever happens. Include the time it was set for and roughly when you noticed. In the meantime, check:

  • Alarm permission. Settings → First Light → make sure alarm scheduling is allowed. Without it, nothing can be scheduled.
  • Ringer volume. Alarms play at your iPhone's ringtone volume. Settings → Sounds & Haptics → turn Ringtone and Alerts up.
  • The alarm is switched on. Open the Alarms tab and check its toggle. To see which days it rings, tap the alarm itself: under REPEAT, the days it repeats on are the filled-in letters.
  • Open the app once after installing an update. It checks and repairs its own schedule every time you open it.

Common questions

Does it work in Silent mode?

Yes. First Light uses Apple's AlarmKit, the same system the built-in Clock app uses, so it rings through Silent mode and Focus.

What is a morning mission?

A small thing you do instead of snoozing: meditation, push-ups, squats, yoga, handstand practice, journalling, reading ten pages, Bible reading, prayer, or a morning walk with a step goal. On Pro you can build your own too. The alarm doesn't go quiet until it's done.

The camera isn't registering my push-ups

Prop the phone where it can see you move, and give it a moment. If it still can't see you, the timer runs anyway after about twenty seconds, and there's a "Just run the timer" button, so you'll never be stuck.

Is my camera footage stored or uploaded?

No. During exercise missions the camera frames are reduced to a coarse brightness pattern to detect movement and then discarded immediately. No video is recorded. Photos you take for a mission stay in the app's private storage on your phone. See the privacy policy.

Can I have more than one alarm?

Yes, with Pro. The free version keeps one alarm and one mission. A Pro subscription lifts the limit: add as many alarms as you like, each with its own time, days, sound and mission. A weekday and a weekend alarm is the common setup.

How do levels and streaks work?

Your level is the number of mornings you've won, and it never resets. Your streak counts consecutive days. Named ranks arrive at 7, 30 and 66 mornings, Riser, Early Riser and First Light, 66 being the average time research suggests a habit takes to become automatic. They keep going after that: Daybreaker at 100, Steadfast at 200, and Year of Mornings at 365.

What happened to my data when I deleted the app?

It's gone. Everything is stored only on your device, so deleting the app deletes your history with it. There's no account and no backup on our side.
